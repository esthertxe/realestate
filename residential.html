Rules to know before you commit

Checked 28 November 2026

  • Stamp duty when you buy

    Buyer's Stamp Duty applies to every purchase. Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty depends on your residency status and how many homes you already own.

  • Seller's Stamp Duty when you sell

    Payable if you sell within 4 years of buying, for homes bought on or after 4 July 2025 (within 3 years for homes bought 11 March 2017 to 3 July 2025). The rate falls the longer you hold.

  • Bank loans

    Banks cap total monthly debt repayments at 55% of income (TDSR). For HDB flats and ECs, the home loan instalment is also capped at 30% of income (MSR). Loan-to-value limits depend on how many housing loans you already have.

  • Renting out

    Private homes must be rented for at least three consecutive months (URA). An HDB flat can be rented out as a whole only after its minimum occupation period, with HDB's approval, for at least six months at a time.

Rules change from time to time. IRAS, MAS, URA and HDB publish the current rules; I will confirm what applies to you.

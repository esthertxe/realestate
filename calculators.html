Seller's Stamp Duty (SSD)

SSD only applies if sold within the holding period (residential: up to 4 years if bought on or after 4 Jul 2025, up to 3 years if bought 11 Mar 2017 to 3 Jul 2025; industrial: up to 3 years). HDB flats sold after the MOP and long-term owners pay none. Commercial (office, retail) has no SSD.

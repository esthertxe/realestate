Rules to know before you commit

Checked 28 November 2026

  • Stamp duty when you buy

    Buyer's Stamp Duty applies; Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty does not apply to commercial property. The top Buyer's Stamp Duty rate for non-residential property is 5%, on the portion above S$1 million.

  • No Seller's Stamp Duty

    Commercial property such as offices and shops is not subject to Seller's Stamp Duty, however long you have owned it.

  • GST

    GST may apply to the purchase price or the rent if the seller or landlord is GST-registered. Check this early, as it affects the cash you need.

  • Approved use

    Check that the unit's approved use, for example office, shop or restaurant, fits what you plan to do there. A change of use may need approval first.

Rules change from time to time. IRAS, MAS, URA and HDB publish the current rules; I will confirm what applies to you.

Rules to know before you commit

Checked 28 November 2026

  • Stamp duty when you buy

    Buyer's Stamp Duty applies; Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty does not apply to industrial property. The top Buyer's Stamp Duty rate for non-residential property is 5%, on the portion above S$1 million.

  • Seller's Stamp Duty when you sell

    Payable if you sell within 3 years of buying: 15% within the first year, 10% in the second and 5% in the third.

  • GST

    GST may apply to the purchase price or the rent if the seller or landlord is GST-registered. Check this early, as it affects the cash you need.

  • Approved use and lease

    The business you run must fit the unit's approved use, and some industrial properties have their own rules on use and subletting. Many industrial properties are on shorter leases than homes, which affects value and financing.

Rules change from time to time. IRAS, MAS, URA and HDB publish the current rules; I will confirm what applies to you.
